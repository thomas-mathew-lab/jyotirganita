# tests/authority/

Worked examples printed in the sources. Each fixture carries its source,
printed page and PDF page, the printed inputs and outputs (each value
with its readings and status, like a datum), and its class:
`text_computed` (worked by the author or a pre-modern commentator in the
text's units, reproduced exactly) or `editor_computed` (a modern
editor's recomputation, compared to the editor's stated precision).
